01 / THE IDEALESS BETWEEN YOU AND YOUR MUSIC

The hardware stays.
The dependency goes.

A dedicated player should start with your library. Not a launcher, an account, or a stack of services that local playback does not need.

I.

Power on.
Press play.

The intended system boots into Rockbox directly. Android is not an intermediate step.

II.

Your files.
Your collection.

Local music and expandable storage. No cloud account or network connection required for core playback.

III.

Open to study.
Built to keep.

Documented source, reproducible builds and an interface that can evolve beyond the stock software.

These are product goals. Better sound, battery life and startup speed remain goals to measure, not results to claim.

THE FOUR DEPLOYMENT DEPENDENCIES

Each must be resolved on the actual platform.

01

Accepted execution

UNRESOLVED

A stock kernel can contain support for non-Android init without accepting a modified ramdisk or root filesystem. The device-specific selection mechanism remains unknown.

Authentication boundaries ↗
02

Selected board resources

UNRESOLVED

The correct DTB/DTBO, matching images, final kernel/module configuration and peripheral firmware cannot be replaced by settings from a related Qualcomm board.

Boot-artifact inventory ↗
03

Safe initialization

UNRESOLVED

The 13-role userspace contract tests prerequisites and failure propagation. It does not supply genuine audio calibration, regulator sequencing or complete thermal and watchdog ownership.

Userspace dependency contract ↗
04

Original-board restoration

UNRESOLVED

A reset, a routine update and replacement of the product are not equivalent to restoring a damaged boot chain on the original board. Restoration must cover the failure states introduced by an experiment.

Recovery readiness ↗
05 / RECOVERABILITYPROTECT THE ORIGINAL MACHINE

A way in.
A way back.

Opening a device is not a reason to lose it. Each failure reaches a different boundary. An available update is not evidence of a surviving recovery route.

Explore the failure model. This is a requirements view, not a recovery tool or a set of device instructions.

06 / THE PATH FORWARDINDEPENDENT RESEARCH. MEASURABLE PROGRESS.

From a working prototype
to a working player.

The next useful step is better evidence from the actual platform, not another generic demo. Device interaction is a separate, bounded investigation.

01

OBSERVE

Read the real system.

Prepare an allowlisted, logged acquisition workflow. Distinguish readable live-tree properties from raw firmware images and keep device data local.

Acquisition tooling is planned, not implemented
02

RESOLVE

Establish the contracts.

Bind boot, board configuration, module readiness, firmware delivery and protection ownership to authentic selected artifacts.

Published sources are not the selected build
03

BRING UP

Make it a music player.

After execution and recovery are established, integrate real input, display, storage and bounded audio. Then measure stability and battery life.

Physical integration has not started
DONE MEANS

Direct boot. Local playback. Physical controls. Safe charging.
